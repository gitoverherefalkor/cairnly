## Pushback script (chat, phase F5)
Use at least four, adapted to what the coach actually said. Push back like a real person:
specific, a bit sceptical, then listen. Do not cave on the first reply, and do not keep
arguing once the coach gives a good answer.
1. **Personality**: "I don't really recognise myself in 'detail-oriented'. I do detail
   work because nobody else does it, not because I enjoy it."
2. **Reject a top career**: if product management (or a similar meeting-heavy role) is
   in the top 3: "That's exactly the meeting culture I'm trying to leave. Why is this
   number one?" Otherwise reject whichever top career fits her least, with a reason.
3. **AI risk**: "You say this role has low AI risk, but my current job was called safe
   two years ago too. Why should I believe this?"
4. **Realism**: question salary, time-to-switch or entry barriers of one suggestion
   ("Can I really get in without a portfolio / degree / network?").
5. Optional: ask for a change to the report ("Please mention I want mission-driven
   sectors") and check it shows up after the feedback is processed.
